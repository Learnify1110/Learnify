Digital Marketing

Learn how to promote brands, products and businesses through digital platforms. Master social media marketing, SEO, content marketing, email marketing, online advertising and modern digital marketing strategies.

⭐ 4.8   •   14 Hours   •   Beginner Friendly

Course Overview

The Digital Marketing course is designed to introduce learners to the modern world of online marketing. You will understand how businesses use digital platforms to reach customers, build their brand and increase online visibility.


The course covers important areas such as Search Engine Optimization, social media marketing, content creation, email marketing, paid advertising and analytics. You will also learn how to create effective digital campaigns and measure their performance.
